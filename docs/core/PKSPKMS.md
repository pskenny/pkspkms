title: PKSPKMS
---

This is a hobby project.

What you can do with it:

- [Query](Querying.md) your [Markdown](Markdown.md) files by their frontmatter (but not full text search)
- Export or query your PKMS (in the command line and web browser)
- Has a concept of "virtual vaults". Other vaults (in the form of directories that can have aliases like `@bob`) can be
  referenced in your vault — see [Virtual PKMS](Virtual%20PKMS.md). Clickable `pkspkms://` links are planned.

See also: [Install](Install.md), [Querying](Querying.md), [Endpoints](Endpoints.md), [Security](Security.md).
